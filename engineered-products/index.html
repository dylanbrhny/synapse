Questions

Questions engineered product makers ask about pay per appointment.

What is pay per appointment cold calling?

A dedicated salesperson cold calls the buyers you define, under your brand, and books them onto your calendar. You buy meetings in bundles and pay per qualified meeting held, not per month.

Do you work with engineered product makers?

Yes. This page is for engineered product makers in the US and Canada. Your salesperson calls OEMs under your brand and books engineering and sourcing decision makers onto your calendar.

How is the price set?

From your close rate, deal size and lifetime value, which say what one held appointment is worth to you. The price is set under that, on the first call, and you pick a bundle size.

How do bundles work?

You buy a bundle of qualified meetings at your per-meeting price. Each meeting held comes off it; no-shows and non-fits don't. When it runs low, top it up or stop.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A decision maker, or someone confirmed in the buying process, at an industrial account on your target list, with a named program at or above your minimum yearly value and a decision expected within 12 months. They picked a specific slot, confirmed their email and accepted the invite, were told who will call and when, and the meeting happened. Anything less is a lead, and leads never come off your bundle.

What happens when a buyer doesn't show?

Nothing comes off your bundle. We rebook the buyer, and the meeting counts only once it's held.

What if a meeting doesn't count?

Raise it with support within 48 hours and it's credited back to your bundle. If the same reason keeps coming up, the definition is tightened.

How do we know the meetings are any good?

Our Synapse Notetaker joins every meeting under your branding and emails you the notes. You see what was said; so do we.

Do your salespeople call under our brand?

Under yours. Your salesperson introduces themselves as part of your company and dials from numbers set up for you.

What does support cover?

Around the clock. A meeting that shouldn't count, a change to your buyer definition, a question about a call: raise it with support at any hour.

Can we cancel?

Whenever you want. No long-term commitment.

Is cold calling lead generation for engineered product makers?

Yes, done by phone. Your salesperson hands you qualified meetings with the engineering and sourcing decision makers on your target list. How it works walks through the process from list to booked meeting.

How do you keep our calls out of Spam Likely?

20 real carrier numbers per salesperson, rotated, and any flagged number replaced the same day.
