The math
Cold calling is arithmetic before it's activity.

Every engagement runs on the same equation. We size it before you sign, not after.

Value =
Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice
Applied to cold calling
Every variable moves in your favor
A dedicated caller raises the outcome (more qualified conversations), raises the certainty (fit-scored targeting instead of a cold list), cuts the delay (live in two weeks), and cuts your effort (your team runs discovery, we run the phones).
The volume math: a steady flow of qualified conversations each month, even at a conservative conversion rate, produces a predictable number of real opportunities. That's not optimism, it's arithmetic, and it's the same math we run before we take on a client.
