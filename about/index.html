Synapse Mfg: dedicated cold calling for industrial manufacturers

Last reviewed September 15, 2026

Synapse Mfg is a cold calling and appointment setting service for industrial manufacturers. It serves owners and sales leads at industrial companies with a proven product and a market large enough to call, who want buyers on the phone this quarter without recruiting, equipping and ramping a caller to reach them.

What does Synapse Mfg do?

Synapse Mfg puts a dedicated salesperson on the phone with your ideal customer profile, under your company name, and books the resulting meetings onto your calendar.

The salesperson works your account and nobody else's. We build the list in Telescope for your ICP and screen it through TitanX before the first dial, so disconnected numbers and dead lines stay out of the queue. The salesperson dials from 20 rotated carrier numbers, so no single line trips the volume filters behind "Spam Likely", and is live inside 14 days of kickoff.

The salesperson logs every conversation. Your dashboard shows which objections come up most, which incumbents hold the accounts on your list, and when contracts and budgets open, updated each week. A month of calling tells you what your market thinks before the appointment count does.

Why manufacturing only?

Industrial buyers ask technical questions inside the first minute, and a salesperson who calls plants all day learns to hold that conversation without posing as the engineer.

A purchasing manager at an OEM wants to know about materials, tolerances, lead times and certifications before agreeing to anything. A generalist caller either loses the thread or bluffs, and both cost the meeting. Our salespeople hold a credible first conversation and book your engineer for the second. The salesperson books the meeting; your engineers carry the technical conversation.

Calling one sector compounds. The objections a spring maker hears in March sound like the ones a mold shop hears in June, and a salesperson who has heard them a thousand times has an answer ready.

How is an engagement run?

Grant, our director, scopes the engagement on a 30-minute call; then a dedicated salesperson calls your market with a manager overseeing the work and a weekly report on what it produced.

The call covers what you sell, who buys it, and whether the market is large enough to keep a salesperson busy. If it isn't, Grant says so on the call. If it is, we build and screen the list, write the opener with you, and go live inside 14 days.

Weekly CRM tracking, QA and reporting run from the first week of conversations. You see conversations, appointments set, objections, incumbents and contract dates on your own dashboard. Appointments land on your calendar or your salesperson's, each one confirmed before it happens. We offer a one-month trial, so both sides can see whether cold calling works for your market before committing to more.

Who has hired Synapse Mfg?

Manufacturers from Italy, Canada, the United States, South Korea, India, Portugal, Germany and Taiwan have hired Synapse Mfg, the overseas ones to enter the North American market.

The accounts they asked us to call range from regional job shops to Fortune 500 plants and government agencies. Our salespeople have set appointments at NASA, the U.S. Department of Energy and the FAA, alongside Fortune 500 manufacturers.

Every case study on the site is anonymized. We describe each client by what it makes and where it sits, and the figures are appointments set, as logged in the program CRM. Read them on the case studies page.
