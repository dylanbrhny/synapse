SMS Messaging Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Overview

Synapse Mfg operates outbound sales programs on behalf of industrial manufacturing clients. Our sales representatives contact prospects via phone, email, and LinkedIn. SMS is used only when a prospect explicitly requests it during an active sales conversation, typically to receive requested materials such as capabilities brochures, spec sheets, or case studies.

How Prospects Opt In

Consent to receive SMS messages from Synapse Mfg is obtained through a clear, two-step verbal-plus-text confirmation process during an active sales conversation:

Opt-In Process

1

Prospect Request

During a phone call, email exchange, or LinkedIn conversation, a prospect explicitly requests that materials be sent via text message (e.g., "text me the brochure" or "send the spec sheet to my mobile").

2

Documented Consent

Our representative records the prospect's name, phone number, date, and method of consent in our internal CRM before any SMS is sent.

3

Confirmation Text

The prospect receives a one-time confirmation text:

Reply YES to confirm you'd like to receive the materials you requested via text from Synapse Mfg. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt-out.
4

Materials Sent

Only after the prospect replies YES does Synapse Mfg send the requested materials via SMS or MMS.

What Prospects Receive

SMS messages from Synapse Mfg are limited to materials a prospect has specifically requested. Examples include:

  • Capabilities brochures and company overviews
  • Product or equipment spec sheets
  • Case studies and reference documents
  • Brief follow-up notes related to materials previously sent

Messaging frequency varies based on the prospect's request and stage in the sales conversation. Synapse Mfg does not send promotional, marketing, or bulk SMS campaigns.

Keyword Responses

REPLY: HELP

Thank you for contacting Synapse Mfg. For support, email grant@synapsemfg.com or visit synapsemfg.com. Reply STOP to opt-out.

REPLY: STOP

You have successfully opted out of messages from Synapse Mfg. You will receive no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe.

Data Privacy

Phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent collected by Synapse Mfg are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in is obtained separately from any other communication channel and is treated as a distinct consent under our Privacy Policy.

Message and Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier and service plan. Synapse Mfg does not charge recipients to receive SMS messages.

Carrier Disclaimer

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Changes to This Policy

Synapse Mfg may update this SMS Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.
