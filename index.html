List intelligence

We spend an unreasonable amount of time keeping bad numbers off the list.

Is this a fit?

We're selective about who we call for.

Cold calling works best for industrial companies with a proven product and a clear buyer. The only missing piece is more conversations with the right people.

Good fit

Industrial companies doing $1M+ in revenue

You have a proven product and existing customers, but your pipeline depends on referrals, trade shows, or the owner's personal network. You want meetings this quarter, not a channel that spends two quarters ramping, and you don't want to hire and manage a salesperson from scratch.
Good fit

Industrial companies selling into enterprise / F500 accounts

Long sales cycles, multiple decision-makers, technical products that require salespeople who actually understand the industrial space. You need someone who can hold a credible phone conversation with engineers and procurement teams and secure the meeting.
Not a fit

Companies with a very small TAM

Cold calling works because there's a large market to dial into. If your total addressable market is a few dozen accounts, you'll exhaust the list fast and need a targeted, relationship-driven approach instead of volume calling.
Not a fit

Early-stage companies without product-market fit

If you're still working out what you sell or who you sell it to, cold calling won't fix that. We work best with companies that know their product works and need more of the right people on the phone.

Questions

Questions manufacturers ask before they outsource cold calling.

Do your salespeople call under our brand or under Synapse?

Under yours. Your salesperson introduces themselves as part of your company, works from a script written with your team, and dials from a bank of 20 carrier numbers provisioned for your engagement. Meetings are booked directly onto your calendar or your salesperson's calendar. Synapse stays in the background.

How is this different from hiring our own BDR?

Hiring one person means recruiting, training, several months of ramp, data contracts, phone numbers, and someone to manage all of it. If that person leaves, the process restarts. Synapse provides the salesperson, the data, the number infrastructure, the dead-number screening, and the management as one engagement, live within 14 days of kickoff.

How is this different from a cold calling agency or an appointment setter?

Most agencies sell leads produced by a shared pool of callers working several clients at once. Synapse assigns a dedicated salesperson to your company, calling only your ICP, under your brand, with a script built around your product. You also receive a dashboard of what the market said: objections, incumbents, timing. We aren't selling you a list of names.

How quickly can calling start?

Under 14 days from kickoff to live calling. Days 1 to 3 cover your product, targets, and script. Days 4 to 7 build and screen the list and provision each salesperson's numbers. The salesperson goes live on the cleaned list in the second week, with manager oversight throughout.

What industries and buyers do you call into?

Industrial and manufacturing companies only. Past engagements include sheet metal fabrication, precision machining, custom springs, tooling and mold, and industrial components and equipment sold into plants. The buyers reached are engineering, purchasing and procurement, plant and operations management, and EHS and industrial hygiene, at companies from regional job shops to Fortune 500 manufacturers. Manufacturers headquartered in Italy, Germany, Portugal, South Korea, India and Taiwan have hired Synapse to win US and Canadian accounts, and Canadian manufacturers have hired Synapse to sell into the United States.

Our target market is small. Does cold calling still work?

If the addressable market is a few dozen accounts, no, and we say so before you book. The model works when there are hundreds to thousands of contactable accounts, or when a smaller number of large accounts each have many buyers to map across engineering, purchasing and production.

What happens on the 30-minute call?

Thirty minutes with Grant, our director, who scopes every engagement. He asks what you sell, who buys it, how new business arrives today, and what your sales organization looks like. You leave with a straight answer on whether cold calling fits your market. If it does, he walks through the first 14 days. No slide deck.

What does it cost?

Engagements are scoped to the size of your market, the hours of outreach, and whether account follow-up is included. Grant scopes it on the call and follows with a written proposal if the model fits.

Do you work on a commission-only basis, or through manufacturers' rep firms?

No to both. Engagements are retainer-based, and we work directly with manufacturers and industrial product companies, not as a downstream channel for manufacturers' rep firms. The two models don't overlap: manufacturers' rep firms sell to the network they already have; they aren't doing cold outbound. Synapse does the opposite: a dedicated salesperson calls the accounts your network has never reached. Stating this up front saves everyone a call.

We already use ZoomInfo or Apollo. Do we still need this?

Tools provide names and numbers. The engagement is the people who work those names on the phone every day, the carrier numbers that keep them out of spam filters, the screening that removes dead records, and the management that keeps the script improving. You can keep your existing tools; the dashboard shows what the calling adds.

How do you keep our calls out of Spam Likely?

Each salesperson dials from 20 real, non-VoIP numbers on Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile, rotated so no single line carries the volume that trips carrier filters. Line reputation is monitored and flagged numbers are replaced the same day.
